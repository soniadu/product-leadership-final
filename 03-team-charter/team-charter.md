# Team Charter: Meridian Foundations

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The Foundations field bet for this quarter, which is the Now-horizon work on field engagement, daily reporting, and schedule changes, plus its success signals, including weekly active field users, the in-platform share of daily logs, and in-app schedule acknowledgment within 24 hours. PM 2 owns the enterprise bets on the roadmap, namely the Next-horizon executive KPI dashboards for the two at-risk renewals and the Later-horizon deeper enterprise platform, along with their renewal and retention signals. Both PMs own their own success signals, and you own the Foundations roadmap as a whole.
>
> **Out of scope:** For PM 1, enterprise feature requests, enterprise integrations, and pricing decisions belong to PM 2, finance and pricing, and enterprise sales respectively. For PM 2, the field app design, field data capture, and mobile engineering priorities belong to PM 1, and the foreman mobile app stays with PM 1 even though it is Later-horizon. Neither PM decides the retention tier's price or boundary alone, since finance and pricing owns that call.
>
> **Joint calls:** Any change that moves engineering capacity between field adoption and enterprise work needs a joint decision from PM 1 and PM 2 before work starts. Any change to the shared data model, including the migration that supports multi-project dashboards, needs joint sign-off because both the field and enterprise bets depend on it. Any enterprise request that claims a renewal at risk must be sized and presented to both PMs before it is accepted onto the roadmap.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** PM 1 approves any scope change that reduces or increases a Now-horizon field item, and PM 2 approves any scope change that adds or reduces enterprise work in the current quarter. PM 2 must also provide a stated OKR or renewal justification for any enterprise scope change that reduces scope or puts PM 1's timeline at risk. You as Foundations owner decide only when the two PMs disagree and the disagreement has gone through escalation.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** Either PM raises a conflict in writing within two business days of noticing it, stating the capacity or scope affected and the OKR or renewal at stake, and the other PM responds within two business days. If they cannot agree within five business days of the initial request, the conflict goes to you as Foundations owner, who makes the final call within three business days, weighted toward the Now-horizon field bet unless the enterprise request protects a renewal named in the at-risk list. Your decision is recorded in the Foundations charter, and any reversal requires the same process.
